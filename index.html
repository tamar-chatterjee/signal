What this emits

Tone — a sine wave at the frequency shown. Phone speakers roll off below about 500 Hz, so anything under that will come out as thin harmonics rather than the fundamental. Use earbuds if you want the low tones properly.

Screen — the panel fills with the selected colour. The three primary chips sit on the display's native red, green and blue emitters; everything between them is a blend of those three that your eye reads as that wavelength, not a single spectral line.

On the water — nothing measurable. Visible light sits in the band where water is most transparent, roughly 0.2% is absorbed passing through a glass, and what is absorbed becomes heat on the order of millionths of a degree. The working parts of this are the pause, the tone as a cue, and the fact that you drink the glass.
